# The Equipment Fund Sprint (July - December 2026)

**The Objective:** Generate $2,500 - $3,000 USD to purchase high-end video editing equipment (e.g., M3 Max MacBook Pro).
**The Constraint:** 12 PM - 9 PM IST Full-Time Job. Low-spec laptop.
**The Strategy:** Sell lightweight Figma-to-Next.js conversions to US SaaS founders. 

## The Daily Schedule

**7:00 AM - 10:30 AM (IST) - The Deep Work Block**
- This is your execution time. 
- When you land a client, you use this time to write the Next.js code and style with Tailwind. 
- *Why:* No distractions, fresh mind, doesn't interfere with your day job.

**12:00 PM - 9:00 PM (IST) - The Day Job**
- Survive. Pay your bills.

**9:30 PM - 11:00 PM (IST) - The Sales Block**
- *Why:* It is 12:00 PM EST in the US. Founders are at their desks.
- **Action:** Record and send 3 Loom videos per night (see script below).

## The Outbound Playbook

### Step 1: Finding Targets
1. Go to **ProductHunt.com** or search Twitter for "#buildinpublic" and "Just launched".
2. Find US/UK based software (SaaS) startups.
3. Open their website. If it feels generic, slow, or lacks the "premium" feel, they are your target.

### Step 2: The Loom Video (The Pitch)
Do not send text emails. Send a 2-minute screen recording.

**The Script:**
> "Hey [Founder Name], congrats on the launch. I'm Trinath, a Creative Developer. I was checking out your site and noticed the hero section isn't converting as well as it could be. I actually took 20 minutes to redesign it in Figma (show your design on screen) to make it feel more premium. If you want me to code this into a lightning-fast Next.js front-end for you, let's chat."

### Step 3: The Closing
When they reply, get them on a Zoom call (schedule it for your 10 PM IST / 12:30 PM EST slot).
- **The Pitch:** "I will rebuild your landing page in Next.js/Tailwind for a flat rate of $1,500 USD. It will be delivered in 7 days."
- **The Payment:** Use Wise or a US Stripe account to accept a 50% deposit upfront.

## The Pivot (End of Year)
Once you hit $3,000 in your separate bank account:
1. Buy the laptop.
2. Stop writing code.
3. Master After Effects & Premiere Pro.
4. Relaunch as a Motion Editor.
