# Business Model

**Phase:** 1 & 2 (Freelancer → Productized Service)
**Status:** Active Execution Playbook

---

## 1. The Core Philosophy
In Phase 1 and 2, the business model is inherently tied to your personal output. 
- **Phase 1 (Freelancer):** You are trading your highly specialized skills (UX/UI, React, AI) for money.
- **Phase 2 (Productized Service):** You are trading a standardized outcome for a fixed price.

Your business model is **NOT** software subscriptions (SaaS) yet. It is high-margin service delivery.

## 2. Phase 1: Freelance Model (Custom Projects)
### Target Audience
Founders, small businesses, and agencies who need custom design and development work but cannot afford full-time talent.

### Value Proposition
"I will act as your elite, on-demand UI/UX designer and React developer to build exactly what you need."

### Revenue Stream
- **Hourly Billing:** For maintenance, consulting, or highly ambiguous tasks.
- **Project Billing (Value-Based):** For custom builds (e.g., "Build an AI Dashboard from scratch").

### Economics
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** Very low (just software subscriptions like Figma, Vercel, OpenAI).
- **Margin:** 90%+
- **Constraint:** Your time. You only have ~100 billable hours a month.

---

## 3. Phase 2: Productized Service Model
### Target Audience
Businesses that need a specific, repeatable problem solved quickly without the overhead of negotiating a custom project scope.

### Value Proposition
"I deliver a specific outcome (e.g., A high-converting React Landing Page with AI integration) in a specific timeframe (7 days) for a specific price."

### Revenue Stream
- **Fixed-Tier Packages:** 
  - *Tier 1 (Basic):* UX Audit & Wireframes ($X,XXX)
  - *Tier 2 (Pro):* Full Design + React Build ($X,XXX)
  - *Tier 3 (Premium):* Design + React + Custom AI Integration ($X,XXX)
- **Ongoing Retainers:** "Unlimited design/dev tasks for $X,XXX/month" (pause or cancel anytime).

### Economics
- **COGS:** Still low, but now includes template costs or Zapier automations.
- **Margin:** 80-90%.
- **Constraint:** Process efficiency. The faster you deliver the standard outcome, the higher your effective hourly rate.

---

## 4. Transition Metrics
You are ready to transition your Business Model from Phase 1 to Phase 2 when:
1. You notice you are solving the *exact same problem* for multiple clients.
2. You are tired of writing custom proposals.
3. You have enough cash flow to turn down bad-fit custom work.
