# Sales Playbook

**Phase:** 1 & 2 (Freelancer → Productized Service)
**Status:** Active Execution Playbook

---

## 1. Sales Philosophy for the Solo Creator
You are an expert consultant, not a desperate order-taker. Your goal on a sales call is to diagnose a business problem and prescribe a technical/design solution. If you cannot help them, you must tell them immediately.

## 2. Inbound Lead Qualification
Do not get on a call with everyone. Protect your time.
Use an intake form (Typeform/Tally) on your website with the following questions:
1. What is the primary goal of this project?
2. What is your ideal timeline?
3. What is your allocated budget for this initiative? (Provide ranges: $2k-$5k, $5k-$10k, $10k+)

*If their budget is below your minimum, send a polite email declining the project before scheduling a call.*

## 3. The 30-Minute Closing Call Script

### Phase 1: The Diagnosis (15 mins)
Let them do 80% of the talking.
- "Tell me about the current state of your product."
- "What is the biggest bottleneck right now?"
- "Why is it important to solve this *now* instead of 6 months from now?"
- "If we build this React feature / AI integration, how does it directly impact your revenue or user growth?"

### Phase 2: The Prescription (10 mins)
Bridge their problem to your skills.
- "Based on what you've said, the core issue is X. The way I solve X is by designing Y and building Z in React."
- Provide a case study: "I recently did exactly this for [Client Name], and it resulted in [Metric]."

### Phase 3: The Budget & Next Steps (5 mins)
Never send a proposal blindly.
- "Typically, an engagement like this ranges between $X and $Y. Does that align with what you had in mind?"
- *If yes:* "Great. I will send over a formal proposal and a payment link by tomorrow morning."
- *If no:* "Understood. At that budget, we could reduce the scope to just [Specific Feature]. Would you like a proposal for that?"

## 4. Handling Objections
- **"You're too expensive."** -> "I understand. My pricing reflects the speed and quality I deliver without you needing to manage me. If budget is the primary concern, I might not be the best fit."
- **"We need this done in 3 days."** -> "I charge a 50% rush fee for expedited timelines. Would you like me to include that in the proposal?"

## 5. Post-Call Process
1. Send proposal within 24 hours.
2. Follow up after 48 hours if unsigned.
3. Once signed, send the 50% upfront invoice immediately. Do not start work until cash is in the bank.
