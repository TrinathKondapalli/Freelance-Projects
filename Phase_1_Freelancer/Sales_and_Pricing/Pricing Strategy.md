# Pricing Strategy

**Phase:** 1 & 2 (Freelancer → Productized Service)
**Status:** Active Execution Playbook

---

## 1. Pricing Philosophy
As a solo creator, your pricing is a reflection of your confidence, the quality of your portfolio, and the ROI you deliver to the client. You are not competing on price; you are competing on quality, speed, and reliability.

## 2. Phase 1: Freelance Pricing
In Phase 1, you must optimize for cash flow and building a world-class portfolio.

### The "Cost-Plus" to "Value-Based" Shift
- **Start Here (Cost-Plus/Hourly):** Determine your minimum acceptable hourly rate based on your living expenses. Multiply by 2 to account for taxes, non-billable admin time, and software costs. (e.g., $75 - $100/hr).
- **Move Here Quickly (Value-Based):** Stop selling hours. Sell the project. If a custom React dashboard takes you 40 hours, but saves the client $50,000 a year in operational costs, price the project at $10,000, not $4,000.

### Rules for Custom Proposals
- Never send a proposal without a budget conversation on the first call.
- Always provide 3 pricing options (The "Anchor", The "Core", The "Basic").
- Require a 50% upfront deposit to begin work.

---

## 3. Phase 2: Productized Service Pricing
In Phase 2, pricing is public, standardized, and non-negotiable.

### The Tiered Model
Package your expertise into distinct, fixed-price tiers.

- **Option A: The One-Off Sprint**
  - *Example:* "AI-Powered Landing Page in 7 Days"
  - *Price:* $3,000 fixed. 100% paid upfront.
  
- **Option B: The Monthly Subscription (Retainer)**
  - *Example:* "Design & Dev Partner Subscription"
  - *Price:* $4,995/month.
  - *Terms:* One request at a time. Pause or cancel anytime. Paid via Stripe subscription on the 1st of the month.

### Rules for Productized Pricing
- No custom quotes. If they want custom, they pay a premium "consulting" rate.
- Increase prices by 10-15% every time you have a waitlist of more than 2 clients.
- Use Stripe Payment Links. Eliminate invoices and accounts receivable chasing.
