# Problem Statement

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Founding Problem Definition

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This document defines the types of problems our company exists to solve.
We do not build products without a validated problem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Belief

Problems create businesses.

Ideas do not.

Our company starts with understanding problems deeply before designing
solutions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problem Criteria

A problem is worth solving only if it is:

-   Real
-   Recurring
-   Expensive
-   Frustrating
-   Experienced by many customers
-   Important enough that customers will pay to solve it

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Initial Industry Focus

Healthcare businesses.

We will begin with one niche (for example, dental clinics) and expand
only after achieving strong product-market fit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problem Categories

## 1. Operational Problems

Examples: - Too many manual tasks - Poor internal workflows - Time
wasted on repetitive work

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Growth Problems

Examples: - Difficulty attracting new customers - Low conversion rates -
Weak online presence - Inefficient follow-up processes

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Customer Experience Problems

Examples: - Confusing booking processes - Poor communication - Long
waiting times - Lack of digital convenience

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Decision-Making Problems

Examples: - Limited business insights - Disconnected information - Poor
reporting

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problem Discovery Process

Every opportunity follows this sequence:

1.  Observe
2.  Interview customers
3.  Validate the pain
4.  Measure business impact
5.  Prioritize
6.  Design the solution

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Questions Before Building

Every problem must answer:

-   Who has this problem?
-   How often does it happen?
-   What does it cost the customer?
-   How is it solved today?
-   Why are existing solutions insufficient?
-   Would customers pay for a better solution?

If these questions cannot be answered with evidence, continue
researching.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Definition

A problem is successfully solved when customers:

-   Save significant time
-   Increase revenue
-   Reduce operational effort
-   Improve customer satisfaction
-   Depend on our solution daily

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problems We Reject

We do not build products for:

-   Temporary trends
-   Nice-to-have features
-   Problems without validation
-   Markets with no willingness to pay
-   Features added only because competitors have them

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Our competitive advantage is not building more software.

Our competitive advantage is understanding customer problems better than
anyone else.
