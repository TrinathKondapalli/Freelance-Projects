# Purpose

This document defines evidence-based customer personas.

Personas are not fictional characters.

They are summaries of patterns observed through interviews, research,
product usage, and customer interactions.

This document must be updated continuously as new evidence is collected.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Persona Creation Rules

Every persona must be supported by:

-   Customer interviews
-   Market research
-   Product usage data
-   Sales conversations
-   Support conversations

Never create a persona based on assumptions.

# Persona Validation Checklist

Before approving a persona:

☐ Interview evidence exists

☐ Goals documented

☐ Problems validated

☐ Buying behavior understood

☐ Decision criteria documented

☐ Real quotes collected

☐ Updated within the last quarter

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Review Schedule

Review every quarter or after significant customer research.

Update based on evidence---not opinions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Know the customer better than they expect.

When we deeply understand the people we serve, building the right
product becomes much easier.
