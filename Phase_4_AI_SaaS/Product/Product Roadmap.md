# The Product Roadmap (Agency to SaaS Transition)

**Phase:** 4 (AI Product Company / SaaS)
**Status:** Active Execution Playbook

---

## 1. The Transition Strategy
The most dangerous time for your business is moving from Phase 3 (Agency cash flow) to Phase 4 (SaaS product). If you abandon the agency too fast, you run out of cash. If you don't dedicate resources to the SaaS, it never launches.

**The Golden Rule:** The SaaS product must solve a problem you deeply understand from your agency clients. Do not build for a market you don't know.

## 2. Step 1: Internal Tooling (The Sandbox)
Before launching the SaaS publicly, build it as an internal tool for your agency.
- **Why?** It forces you to use the product daily, guarantees immediate ROI (makes your agency faster), and tests the architecture without public embarrassment.
- **Goal:** Get your internal agency team using the tool for 30 days straight.

## 3. Step 2: The Beta Cohort (The Transition)
Once the internal tool is solid, offer it to your existing/past agency clients for free or at a steep discount.
- **Why?** You already have their trust. They will give you brutal, honest feedback.
- **Goal:** Convert 5 agency clients into daily active SaaS users.

## 4. Step 3: Public Launch & Resource Shift
Once the Beta cohort proves retention, you launch publicly.
At this stage, you begin shifting resources:
- Redirect 50% of the agency profit into SaaS marketing.
- Move your best developer from client work to full-time SaaS engineering.
- Cap agency growth. Stop taking new custom clients unless the retainer is massive.

## 5. Managing the Roadmap (Now, Next, Later)
Do not use timeline-based roadmaps (e.g., "Feature X in Q3"). They are always wrong. Use thematic roadmaps.
- **Now:** What are we actively coding this sprint? (e.g., Stripe integration, core AI prompt optimization).
- **Next:** What is being designed and researched for the upcoming sprints? (e.g., User management, Zapier integration).
- **Later:** Big ideas that fit the vision but lack definition. (e.g., Native iOS app).
