# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Founding GTM Strategy

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This document defines how we introduce products to the market, acquire
our first customers, validate product-market fit, and scale sustainably.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# GTM Philosophy

We do not launch to everyone.

We launch to one clearly defined customer segment, solve one important
problem, and expand only after proven success.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Stage 1 --- Research

Objectives: - Understand the industry. - Interview potential
customers. - Validate the problem. - Analyze competitors.

Deliverable: A validated problem worth solving.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Stage 2 --- Early Relationships

Objectives: - Build trust. - Share insights. - Demonstrate expertise. -
Collect feedback.

Channels: - LinkedIn - Industry networking - Direct outreach - Referrals

Success Metric: Meaningful customer conversations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Stage 3 --- MVP Launch

Objectives: - Solve one validated problem. - Onboard a small group of
early adopters. - Measure real usage.

Focus: - Learning over revenue. - Feedback over feature requests.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Stage 4 --- Product-Market Fit

Indicators:

-   Customers actively use the product.
-   Customers renew subscriptions.
-   Customers recommend us.
-   Customer retention improves.
-   Organic referrals increase.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Stage 5 --- Scale

Expand through:

-   Content marketing
-   SEO
-   Customer referrals
-   Strategic partnerships
-   Case studies
-   Paid acquisition (only after proven economics)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Ideal Customer Journey

1.  Discover us.
2.  Understand the value.
3.  Request a demo or trial.
4.  Experience measurable results.
5.  Become a paying customer.
6.  Renew.
7.  Recommend us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# GTM Decision Checklist

Before launch, confirm:

-   The problem is validated.
-   The ICP is clearly defined.
-   The value proposition is simple.
-   Success metrics are defined.
-   Customer onboarding is ready.
-   Support is prepared.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Key Metrics

Track:

-   Qualified Leads
-   Demo Requests
-   Trial Signups
-   Trial-to-Paid Conversion
-   Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
-   Payback Period
-   Customer Retention
-   Referral Rate

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mistakes We Avoid

-   Launching without validation.
-   Targeting multiple industries at once.
-   Competing only on price.
-   Building features nobody requested.
-   Scaling before product-market fit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Earn trust first.

Validate continuously.

Scale only after customers consistently achieve meaningful outcomes.
