# Purpose

This playbook defines how we attract, educate, convert, retain, and
delight customers. Marketing exists to create trust, communicate value,
and drive sustainable business growth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Marketing Philosophy

We do not market products.

We market customer outcomes.

Our objective is to become the most trusted source of knowledge in our
market.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Growth Principles

-   Educate before selling.
-   Build trust before asking for commitment.
-   Solve problems publicly.
-   Measure everything.
-   Optimize continuously.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Growth Funnel

1.  Awareness
2.  Interest
3.  Consideration
4.  Conversion
5.  Activation
6.  Retention
7.  Advocacy

Every campaign must support at least one stage.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Target Audience

Define for every campaign:

-   Ideal Customer Profile
-   Persona
-   Customer Problem
-   Desired Outcome
-   Buying Stage

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Marketing Channels

Owned: - Website - Blog - Email - Product

Earned: - Referrals - Reviews - PR - Community

Shared: - LinkedIn - X - YouTube - Instagram

Paid: - Search Ads - Social Ads - Retargeting

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Messaging Framework

Always communicate:

1.  Customer Problem
2.  Business Impact
3.  Our Solution
4.  Proof
5.  Call to Action

Benefits before features.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Campaign Framework

Every campaign includes:

-   Goal
-   Audience
-   Message
-   Offer
-   Channel
-   Landing Page
-   Success Metrics
-   Review Date

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Content Strategy

Create content that:

-   Answers customer questions
-   Demonstrates expertise
-   Shares case studies
-   Explains industry trends
-   Builds long-term trust

Avoid clickbait and exaggerated claims.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Customer Proof

Prioritize:

-   Testimonials
-   Case Studies
-   Product Demos
-   Success Metrics
-   Customer Stories

Evidence builds credibility.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Marketing KPIs

Track:

-   Website Traffic
-   Qualified Leads
-   Conversion Rate
-   Customer Acquisition Cost
-   Customer Lifetime Value
-   Email Engagement
-   Organic Traffic
-   Referral Rate
-   Marketing ROI

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Experimentation

Every month:

-   Test one headline
-   Test one landing page
-   Test one CTA
-   Test one acquisition channel

Document learnings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Review Checklist

☐ Campaign objective defined

☐ Audience validated

☐ Brand guidelines followed

☐ Metrics configured

☐ Landing page reviewed

☐ Analytics enabled

☐ Follow-up plan prepared

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Selling too early
-   Inconsistent messaging
-   Ignoring analytics
-   Chasing every platform
-   Measuring vanity metrics

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Great marketing earns attention by creating value.

When customers consistently learn from us, they naturally trust us,
recommend us, and choose our products.
