# Social Media Strategy

**Version:** 1.0 **Status:** Founding Social Growth Operating System
**Parent Documents:** Brand Guidelines.md, Marketing Playbook.md,
Content Strategy.md

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This document defines how we use social media to educate, build trust,
grow our audience, strengthen our brand, and generate qualified business
opportunities.

Social media is a long-term relationship channel---not a broadcasting
platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Social Media Philosophy

Create value before asking for attention.

Teach more than you promote.

Earn trust through consistency, expertise, and authenticity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Objectives

-   Build brand awareness
-   Demonstrate expertise
-   Educate the market
-   Generate qualified leads
-   Build community
-   Support customer success
-   Strengthen founder and company credibility

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Platform Strategy

Use each platform for its strengths.

LinkedIn: - Professional insights - Case studies - Founder thought
leadership

YouTube: - Tutorials - Product demos - Deep educational videos

X: - Industry discussions - Product updates - Quick insights

Instagram: - Visual storytelling - Short-form education -
Behind-the-scenes

Prioritize quality over platform quantity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Content Pillars

-   Industry Education
-   Customer Problems
-   Product Education
-   Case Studies
-   Tutorials
-   Founder Journey
-   AI & Technology
-   Company Updates

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Brand Voice

Always be:

-   Helpful
-   Honest
-   Professional
-   Curious
-   Respectful

Never use fear, hype, or misleading claims.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Content Workflow

1.  Research
2.  Plan
3.  Create
4.  Review
5.  Design
6.  Publish
7.  Engage
8.  Measure
9.  Repurpose
10. Improve

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Repurposing Strategy

Transform one idea into:

-   Long-form article
-   LinkedIn post
-   X thread
-   YouTube video
-   Instagram carousel
-   Newsletter
-   Short video

Create once. Distribute many times.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Community Engagement

-   Respond promptly
-   Answer questions thoughtfully
-   Encourage discussion
-   Celebrate customer success
-   Listen before speaking

Build relationships, not followers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# AI-Assisted Workflow

AI may assist with:

-   Research
-   Brainstorming
-   Captions
-   Drafts
-   Repurposing

Human review is required before publishing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Publishing Standards

Every post should include:

-   Clear purpose
-   Customer value
-   Brand consistency
-   Accessible formatting
-   Call to action when appropriate

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Metrics

Track:

-   Reach
-   Engagement Rate
-   Follower Growth
-   Saves
-   Shares
-   Comments
-   Click-Through Rate
-   Qualified Leads
-   Conversion Rate

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Monthly Review

Review:

-   Top-performing content
-   Audience feedback
-   Platform growth
-   Content gaps
-   New experiments

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Posting without strategy
-   Chasing trends
-   Inconsistent publishing
-   Ignoring comments
-   Measuring vanity metrics only

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Social media should make our company the most trusted voice in our
niche.

Every post should educate, build trust, and move customers one step
closer to success.
