# Risk Management Framework

**Version:** 1.0 **Status:** Founding Operational Risk Operating System
**Parent Documents:** Operations Philosophy.md, Project Management
Framework.md, Decision Log.md

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Define how risks are identified, evaluated, mitigated, monitored, and
reviewed across the company.

Risk management protects customers, the business, and long-term growth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk Philosophy

Do not wait for problems to happen.

Identify risks early, reduce their likelihood, minimize their impact,
and learn from every incident.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk Principles

-   Prevention over reaction
-   Evidence-based assessment
-   Clear ownership
-   Continuous monitoring
-   Transparent communication
-   Continuous improvement

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk Categories

Evaluate risks across:

-   Strategic
-   Product
-   Technology
-   Security
-   Financial
-   Legal & Compliance
-   Operational
-   Customer Success
-   Reputation
-   Third-Party / Vendor

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk Assessment Matrix

Assess every risk using:

-   Probability
    -   Low
    -   Medium
    -   High
-   Impact
    -   Low
    -   Medium
    -   High

Priority = Probability × Impact

Address High/High risks first.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk Register

Each risk must include:

-   Risk ID
-   Title
-   Category
-   Description
-   Owner
-   Probability
-   Impact
-   Priority
-   Current Status
-   Review Date

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mitigation Plan

For each risk define:

-   Preventive actions
-   Detection method
-   Response plan
-   Contingency plan
-   Recovery plan

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Incident Response

When an incident occurs:

1.  Detect
2.  Assess
3.  Contain
4.  Communicate
5.  Resolve
6.  Recover
7.  Review
8.  Improve

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Continuity

Maintain plans for:

-   System failures
-   Data loss
-   Security incidents
-   Service outages
-   Key personnel absence
-   Critical vendor failure

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Escalation

Escalate immediately when:

-   Customer data is at risk
-   Security is compromised
-   Major deadlines are threatened
-   Financial impact is significant
-   Brand reputation is affected

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Monitoring

Review:

-   Open risks
-   New risks
-   Incident trends
-   Mitigation progress
-   Control effectiveness

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk KPIs

Track:

-   Open High Risks
-   Incident Frequency
-   Mean Time to Detect
-   Mean Time to Resolve
-   SLA Compliance
-   Customer Impact
-   Financial Loss Avoided

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Post-Incident Review

Document:

-   Timeline
-   Root Cause
-   Business Impact
-   Response Effectiveness
-   Lessons Learned
-   Process Improvements

Focus on learning, not blame.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Review Cadence

Weekly: - Critical risks

Monthly: - Risk register

Quarterly: - Company-wide risk assessment

Annually: - Business continuity review

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk Checklist

☐ Risk identified ☐ Owner assigned ☐ Impact assessed ☐ Mitigation
defined ☐ Contingency prepared ☐ Review scheduled ☐ Lessons documented

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Ignoring early warning signs
-   No ownership
-   No contingency planning
-   Treating risks as one-time events
-   Failing to learn from incidents

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Effective risk management is not about eliminating all uncertainty.

It is about making informed decisions, preparing for change, and
protecting the company's ability to serve customers over the long term.
