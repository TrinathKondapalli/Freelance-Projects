# Market Research Framework

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Founding Research Framework

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This framework defines how we research markets before committing time,
money, or engineering resources.

Our goal is to reduce uncertainty through evidence---not assumptions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Research Philosophy

Never build based on intuition alone.

Research first.

Validate second.

Build third.

Every major business decision should begin with structured market
research.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Research Objectives

Every research project must answer:

-   Is the market worth entering?
-   Is the problem important?
-   Who experiences it?
-   How are people solving it today?
-   Where are existing solutions failing?
-   Can we create a meaningful competitive advantage?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Market Research Process

## Stage 1 --- Define the Market

Identify:

-   Industry
-   Customer segment
-   Geographic market
-   Business size
-   Decision makers

Deliverable: A clearly defined target market.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 2 --- Understand the Market

Research:

-   Industry trends
-   Market size
-   Growth rate
-   Digital adoption
-   Regulations
-   AI opportunities

Deliverable: Market Overview Report.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 3 --- Understand Customers

Discover:

-   Daily workflows
-   Biggest frustrations
-   Current tools
-   Purchasing behavior
-   Success metrics

Methods:

-   Interviews
-   Surveys
-   Observation
-   Online communities
-   Review analysis

Deliverable: Customer Insights Report.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 4 --- Study Competitors

Analyze:

-   Products
-   Pricing
-   Positioning
-   Features
-   Strengths
-   Weaknesses
-   Customer reviews

Questions:

-   Why do customers choose them?
-   Why do customers leave them?
-   What opportunities exist?

Deliverable: Competitive Landscape Report.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 5 --- Opportunity Analysis

Evaluate:

-   Problem importance
-   Customer willingness to pay
-   Market competition
-   Technical feasibility
-   Long-term scalability

Decision:

-   Proceed
-   Research Further
-   Reject

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Research Checklist

Before building anything, confirm:

☐ Market defined

☐ Problem validated

☐ Customer interviewed

☐ Competitors analyzed

☐ Willingness to pay confirmed

☐ Opportunity documented

☐ Strategic fit confirmed

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Evidence Sources

Preferred:

-   Customer interviews
-   Product demos
-   Customer reviews
-   Industry reports
-   Government publications
-   Public financial reports
-   Product analytics

Avoid relying only on opinions or social media trends.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Research Deliverables

Every research project should produce:

1.  Market Summary
2.  Customer Insights
3.  Competitor Analysis
4.  Opportunity Assessment
5.  Risks
6.  Recommendations
7.  Next Actions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Metrics

Research is successful when it reduces uncertainty and provides enough
evidence to make a confident business decision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Researching without a goal.
-   Talking only to existing customers.
-   Ignoring competitors.
-   Assuming demand.
-   Building before validation.
-   Collecting data without drawing conclusions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Research is not a formality.

It is the foundation of every successful product we build.
