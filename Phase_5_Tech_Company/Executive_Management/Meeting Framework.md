# Executive Meeting Framework

**Phase:** 5 (Technology Company)
**Status:** Active Execution Playbook

---

## 1. The Role of the CEO
In Phase 5, the CEO (You) does not attend daily standups. You do not review pull requests. You do not manage junior employees.
Your job is to manage the executive team (VP of Engineering, VP of Sales, Head of Product). 
Your output is Capital Allocation, Vision Alignment, and Culture.

## 2. The Board Meeting (Monthly/Quarterly)
**Purpose:** Fiduciary duty, high-level financial review, and macro strategy.
**Attendees:** CEO, CFO, Board of Directors, Key Investors.
**Structure:**
- *Pre-read:* A 10-page memo sent 48 hours in advance. No presenting numbers on the call.
- *Agenda:* 
  1. Quick review of KPIs (EBITDA, Churn, ARR).
  2. The "One Big Thing" (Deep dive into the biggest strategic challenge or opportunity).
  3. Approvals (M&A, Executive Hires, Budget changes).

## 3. The Executive Leadership Meeting (Weekly)
**Purpose:** Unblock department heads and ensure cross-functional alignment.
**Attendees:** CEO and Direct Reports (VPs / C-Suite).
**Structure:**
- *Scorecard Review:* 5 minutes reviewing the top 10 company KPIs.
- *Customer/Market Update:* 5 minutes on what we are hearing from the market.
- *Issues (IDS - Identify, Discuss, Solve):* 45 minutes focused exclusively on solving inter-departmental bottlenecks. (e.g., "Sales is selling features Product hasn't built yet").

## 4. The Skip-Level Meeting (Bi-Weekly)
**Purpose:** The CEO must stay connected to the ground truth. Middle managers often filter bad news.
**Attendees:** CEO and a rotating group of 3-4 individual contributors (engineers, sales reps).
**Structure:**
- "What is the most frustrating part of your day?"
- "If you were CEO for a week, what rule would you change?"
- "Is the company vision clear to you?"

## 5. The Golden Rule of Meetings at Scale
No meeting can exist without a specific agenda and an owner. If a meeting can be replaced by a well-written memo, cancel the meeting.
