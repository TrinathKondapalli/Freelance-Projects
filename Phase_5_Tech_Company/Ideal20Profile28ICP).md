# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Founding ICP

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This document defines our ideal customer. We optimize our products,
marketing, sales, and support for this customer before expanding to
additional markets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Principle

We do not build for everyone.

We build for one customer exceptionally well.

Focus creates expertise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Initial Target Industry

Healthcare

Our first target niche should be a single healthcare segment (for
example, dental clinics). We will expand only after achieving strong
product-market fit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Ideal Customer Characteristics

The ideal customer:

-   Has recurring business or operational problems.
-   Is actively looking for better solutions.
-   Is willing to invest to improve outcomes.
-   Values technology and efficiency.
-   Wants long-term growth rather than quick fixes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Profile

Typical customer:

-   Small to mid-sized healthcare business
-   Growing organization
-   Decision-maker involved in daily operations
-   Open to digital transformation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Makers

We primarily communicate with:

-   Founder
-   Owner
-   Clinic Director
-   Practice Manager
-   Operations Manager

Our messaging should focus on business outcomes rather than technical
features.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Primary Goals

Our customers want to:

-   Save time
-   Increase revenue
-   Reduce manual work
-   Improve customer experience
-   Simplify operations
-   Make better business decisions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Primary Pain Points

Examples include:

-   Inefficient workflows
-   Repetitive manual tasks
-   Poor digital experiences
-   Difficulty attracting or retaining customers
-   Lack of operational visibility

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Buying Motivation

Customers purchase when they believe our solution will:

-   Produce measurable results
-   Reduce risk
-   Save staff time
-   Improve profitability
-   Be easy to adopt

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Definition

We are successful when our customer says:

-   "Our team works more efficiently."
-   "We save significant time."
-   "Our business performs better."
-   "We rely on this product every day."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# ICP Review Process

Review and update this document regularly based on:

-   Customer interviews
-   Product usage
-   Sales conversations
-   Market research
-   Support feedback

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Know one customer better than anyone else.

When we deeply understand one customer, building the right product
becomes much easier.
