# Revenue Model

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Founding Revenue Strategy

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This document defines how the company earns, grows, and sustains
revenue. Revenue is the outcome of delivering consistent customer value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Revenue Philosophy

We do not optimize for one-time payments.

We optimize for long-term customer relationships and recurring revenue.

Our objective is to build a predictable, profitable, and scalable
business.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Revenue Evolution

## Stage 1 --- Services

Purpose: - Learn the industry - Validate problems - Generate early cash
flow - Build customer trust

Revenue Sources: - Consulting - Product Design - Development -
Implementation

Success Metric: - Customer learning, not maximum profit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 2 --- Product

Purpose: - Turn repeatable services into software.

Revenue Sources: - MVP licensing - Pilot programs - Early adopters

Success Metric: - Product-Market Fit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 3 --- SaaS

Purpose: - Build recurring revenue.

Revenue Sources: - Monthly subscriptions - Annual subscriptions - Team
plans - Enterprise plans

Success Metric: - Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Revenue Streams

Primary: - SaaS Subscription

Secondary: - Onboarding - Data Migration - Staff Training - Premium
Support - Enterprise Integrations

Future: - AI Premium Features - API Access - Marketplace Integrations

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Revenue Principles

-   Customers pay for outcomes.
-   Simple pricing beats complex pricing.
-   Increase value before increasing price.
-   Earn renewals through customer success.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Key Metrics

Track:

-   MRR
-   ARR
-   Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
-   Lifetime Value (LTV)
-   LTV:CAC Ratio
-   Gross Margin
-   Churn Rate
-   Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
-   Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Healthy Targets

-   Gross Margin: \>70%
-   LTV:CAC: 3:1 or higher
-   Annual Churn: As low as practical
-   Customer Retention: Continuously improve

Targets evolve as the business matures.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Pricing Review Checklist

Before changing pricing:

-   Are customers receiving more value?
-   Is customer retention healthy?
-   Is support sustainable?
-   Is the pricing easy to understand?
-   Does pricing align with our positioning?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Revenue Decisions We Avoid

-   One-time customization as the core business.
-   Hidden fees.
-   Pricing that confuses customers.
-   Discounting without strategic reason.
-   Building features solely to justify higher prices.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Our revenue grows because our customers continue to receive value---not
because we continually sell to new customers.
