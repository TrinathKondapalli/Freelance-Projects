# Problem Validation Framework

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Founding Validation Framework

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This framework ensures we validate a problem before investing in design,
development, or marketing.

Our goal is to build products customers genuinely need.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Principle

Never validate an idea.

Validate the problem.

Ideas change.

Real customer problems remain.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Validation Criteria

A problem is worth solving only if it is:

-   Real
-   Frequent
-   Painful
-   Expensive
-   Experienced by a defined customer group
-   Important enough that customers will pay for a better solution

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Validation Process

## Stage 1 --- Define the Problem

Document:

-   Problem statement
-   Target customer
-   Current workflow
-   Current alternatives
-   Business impact

Deliverable: Problem Definition.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 2 --- Customer Discovery

Interview at least 15--30 potential customers.

Understand:

-   Daily workflow
-   Biggest frustrations
-   Existing solutions
-   Desired outcomes

Avoid leading questions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 3 --- Evidence Collection

Collect evidence from:

-   Customer interviews
-   Product reviews
-   Support forums
-   Industry reports
-   Sales conversations
-   Observations

Evidence should outweigh assumptions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 4 --- Measure the Pain

Ask:

-   How often does this happen?
-   How much time is lost?
-   How much money is lost?
-   What happens if nothing changes?
-   How urgent is the problem?

Score severity from 1--10.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 5 --- Willingness to Pay

Confirm:

-   Have customers already paid for alternatives?
-   Would they switch?
-   Under what conditions?
-   What result would justify paying?

If nobody is willing to pay, continue researching.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stage 6 --- Build the Smallest Test

Before building a full product:

-   Prototype
-   Landing page
-   Manual service
-   Demo
-   Concierge MVP

Learn before scaling.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Validation Checklist

Before approving development:

☐ Problem clearly defined

☐ Customer segment identified

☐ Interviews completed

☐ Evidence documented

☐ Pain measured

☐ Willingness to pay confirmed

☐ Small experiment completed

☐ Results reviewed

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Validation Scorecard

Rate (1--10):

-   Problem Severity
-   Frequency
-   Customer Urgency
-   Willingness to Pay
-   Market Demand
-   Strategic Fit
-   Confidence Level

Decision:

55--70: Build MVP

40--54: Validate Further

Below 40: Reject or Archive

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Falling in love with the solution.
-   Interviewing too few customers.
-   Asking leading questions.
-   Assuming interest equals demand.
-   Building before validation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Metrics

Validation is successful when:

-   Multiple customers describe the same problem.
-   Customers can quantify its impact.
-   Customers express willingness to pay.
-   Evidence supports moving to an MVP.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

The cost of validation is small.

The cost of building the wrong product is enormous.

Validate first. Build second.
