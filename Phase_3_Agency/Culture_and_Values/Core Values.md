# Purpose

Our core values are permanent. They guide every decision, product,
customer interaction, hiring choice, and long-term strategy.

These values do not change with trends, technology, or market
conditions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Customer Success First

We succeed only when our customers succeed.

Every decision should improve customer outcomes, not just our metrics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Solve Real Problems

We build solutions for validated, meaningful, and recurring problems.

We never build products only because the technology is interesting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Integrity Always

We are honest with customers, partners, and ourselves.

We never overpromise or mislead.

Trust is our most valuable asset.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Excellence Over Perfection

We aim for high-quality execution while continuously improving.

Progress matters, but quality is never ignored.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Simplicity Wins

Simple products are easier to learn, trust, maintain, and scale.

We remove unnecessary complexity whenever possible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Learn Continuously

We stay curious.

We learn from customers, data, failures, competitors, and new
technologies.

Learning is part of our daily work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Think Long Term

We optimize for lasting value instead of quick wins.

Every major decision should strengthen the company five or ten years
from now.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Responsible Innovation

We embrace AI and new technologies only when they create meaningful
value.

Innovation must improve people's work---not create unnecessary
complexity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Ownership

Everyone acts like an owner.

We take responsibility for our decisions, our work, and our results.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Discipline and Focus

We protect our time, attention, and priorities.

We say "no" to distractions so we can build exceptional products.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Value Check

Before making an important decision, ask:

-   Does this help our customers?
-   Is it honest?
-   Is it simple?
-   Does it create long-term value?
-   Would we still be proud of this decision years from now?

If not, reconsider.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Commitment

Our values define who we are when no one is watching.

Products may evolve.

Technology will change.

Our values will remain constant.
