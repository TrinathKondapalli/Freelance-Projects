# Agency Hiring Principles

**Phase:** 3 (Agency)
**Status:** Active Execution Playbook

---

## 1. Hiring Philosophy
In Phase 3, you transition from being the primary executor to the manager of executors. Your product is no longer the code or the design; your product is your *team* and the *system*. 

Hire slow, fire fast, and hire for specific, systematized roles. Do not hire "jacks of all trades" (that's your job). Hire specialists who can execute one of your SOPs better and faster than you.

## 2. The Hiring Sequence
Do not hire full-time W2 employees immediately. Build the agency team in this order:

1. **The Fulfillment Contractor (Freelancer to Freelancer):** 
   - Hire a junior React dev or UI designer on Upwork/Fiverr to handle 80% of a project using your boilerplate/Figma templates. You act as the QA (Quality Assurance) layer and handle the final 20% polish.
2. **The Project Manager (Part-time):**
   - Hire a virtual assistant or junior PM to handle client communications, weekly updates, and moving cards in Linear/Trello. This removes you from the client communication bottleneck.
3. **The Full-Time Specialist:**
   - Once contractor costs exceed the cost of a full-time salary (and cash flow is stable), hire your first full-time developer or designer.

## 3. The Interview Process (For Technical Roles)
Do not rely on resumes. Rely on paid trials.

1. **The Filter:** A simple Google Form asking for portfolio links and 3 specific technical questions. (Filters out 80% of bad fits).
2. **The Culture Fit Call (15 mins):** Are they a clear communicator? Do they have a fast internet connection? Are they reliable?
3. **The Paid Trial (Crucial Step):** Pay your top 3 candidates to complete a small, isolated ticket from an actual project using your SOPs. 
4. **The Hire:** Hire the one who followed the SOP best, asked the smartest questions, and delivered on time.

## 4. Onboarding Expectation
A new hire will slow you down for the first 30 days. This is normal. 
- Week 1: They watch you work and read SOPs.
- Week 2: They do the work, you heavily review and correct.
- Week 3: They do the work, you do standard QA.
- Week 4: They operate autonomously within their specific role.
