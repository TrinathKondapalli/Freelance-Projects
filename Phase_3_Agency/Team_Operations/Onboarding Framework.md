**Version:** 1.0 **Status:** Founding Employee Success Operating System
**Parent Documents:** Hiring Principles.md, Company Handbook.md,
Operations Philosophy.md

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Define how every new team member becomes aligned, productive, and
successful through a structured onboarding experience.

Great onboarding accelerates confidence, performance, and long-term
retention.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Onboarding Philosophy

Onboarding is not orientation.

It is the process of helping people understand our mission, culture,
systems, customers, and expectations while enabling them to contribute
meaningful value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Onboarding Principles

-   Customer impact first
-   Learning through real work
-   Clear expectations
-   Early feedback
-   Continuous support
-   Documentation-first
-   Ownership from day one

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Goals

Every new team member should:

-   Understand the company mission
-   Know their role and responsibilities
-   Access required tools
-   Meet key teammates
-   Deliver an initial contribution
-   Receive meaningful feedback

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 30--60--90 Day Framework

## First 30 Days

-   Company orientation
-   Tool setup
-   Product training
-   Team introductions
-   Shadow experienced teammates
-   Complete first small project

## Days 31--60

-   Own recurring responsibilities
-   Deepen product knowledge
-   Improve workflow efficiency
-   Participate in planning and reviews

## Days 61--90

-   Independently manage core responsibilities
-   Contribute improvements
-   Mentor newer teammates when appropriate
-   Agree on long-term development goals

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# First Day Checklist

-   Welcome session
-   Equipment & account setup
-   Security briefing
-   Company handbook review
-   Team introductions
-   Manager meeting
-   Success expectations

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Role-Specific Plan

Every role includes:

-   Learning objectives
-   Required documentation
-   Training resources
-   Practice assignments
-   Success milestones

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Buddy System

Assign a buddy to:

-   Answer questions
-   Explain workflows
-   Introduce culture
-   Reduce onboarding friction

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Training Roadmap

Provide training for:

-   Products
-   Customers
-   Processes
-   Tools
-   Security
-   Communication
-   Quality standards

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Feedback Checkpoints

Manager check-ins:

-   End of Week 1
-   Day 30
-   Day 60
-   Day 90

Discuss progress, blockers, and development.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Metrics

Track:

-   Time to productivity
-   Training completion
-   New hire satisfaction
-   Early performance
-   Retention
-   Manager feedback

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risks

Watch for:

-   Unclear expectations
-   Missing access
-   Information overload
-   Poor communication
-   Lack of feedback

Address issues early.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Review & Improvement

Quarterly:

-   Review onboarding outcomes
-   Update materials
-   Improve training
-   Gather new hire feedback

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Onboarding Checklist

☐ Accounts created ☐ Tools configured ☐ Training completed ☐ Buddy
assigned ☐ Manager check-ins scheduled ☐ Initial goals defined ☐ First
contribution delivered

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Successful onboarding transforms new hires into confident contributors
by combining clear expectations, continuous support, structured
learning, and meaningful early wins.
