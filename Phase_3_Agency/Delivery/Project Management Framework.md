# Agency Project Management Framework

**Phase:** 3 (Agency)
**Status:** Active Execution Playbook

---

## 1. The Multi-Client Challenge
In Phase 1 and 2, you could keep project details in your head. In Phase 3, keeping details in your head will cause your agency to collapse. When managing 5+ active client projects and a team of 3+ contractors, you need a single source of truth.

## 2. The Core Tools
- **The Tracker:** Linear, Jira, or a highly structured Notion database.
- **The Comm Channel:** Slack (Internal team only) + Email/Shared Slack channel (Client).
- **The Knowledge Base:** Notion or Google Docs (for SOPs and client briefs).

## 3. The Agile-Lite Methodology
Do not overcomplicate with strict Scrum ceremonies. Use a simple Kanban + Sprint model.

### Board Structure (Per Project)
- **Backlog:** Everything that needs to be done.
- **Up Next (This Sprint):** Tasks assigned for the current week.
- **In Progress:** Max 2 tickets per team member.
- **Internal QA:** Assigned back to the Founder/Lead for review against standards.
- **Client Review:** Waiting on client feedback.
- **Done:** Deployed and approved.

## 4. The Daily/Weekly Rhythm

### Daily: Async Standup
Every morning, the team posts in Slack:
1. What I completed yesterday.
2. What I am working on today.
3. Blockers (What is preventing me from moving forward).

### Weekly: Sprint Planning & Pipeline Review (Mondays)
- **Founder & PM:** Review all active projects.
- Pull tasks from the Backlog into "Up Next" based on client priorities and team capacity.
- Ensure every ticket has clear acceptance criteria. A developer should never have to guess what "done" looks like.

### Weekly: Client Updates (Fridays)
- PM sends the standardized Weekly Update SOP (created in Phase 2) to all active clients. 

## 5. Handling Scope Creep at Scale
When a client asks for "one small extra feature":
1. **The PM intercepts:** The developer NEVER says yes. The PM says, "Great idea, let me estimate the effort."
2. **Evaluate:** Does this fit in the current sprint?
3. **Execute or Quote:** If it takes < 1 hour, do it as a bonus. If > 1 hour, send a change order with a price tag and timeline impact.
